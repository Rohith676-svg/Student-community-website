# STC — Student Technology Community

> **Different interests. One community.**

STC (Student Technology Community) is a student-led technology community built to bring curious students together to **learn, build, connect, and grow**.

The community creates a space where students can explore technology, work on projects, discover events and hackathons, follow structured learning roadmaps, and connect with people who share similar interests.

---

## What is STC?

Technology is easier to learn when you don't learn it alone.

STC brings students with different interests and skill levels into one community — giving them opportunities to learn from each other, build real projects, participate in events and hackathons, discover opportunities, and grow beyond the classroom.

### Learn

Discover something new from the people around you.

### Build

Turn what you learn into something real.

### Connect

Find people who are building toward the same things.

### Grow

Grow together, beyond the classroom.

---

## Website

The STC website acts as the digital home of the community.

It brings together:

- 🏠 **Home** — Learn what STC is about
- 👥 **Leads** — Meet the students behind the community
- 📅 **Events** — Discover STC events and activities
- 🚀 **Hackathons** — Explore external hackathons and opportunities
- 🧭 **Roadmaps** — Follow curated learning paths
- 🤝 **Join** — Become part of the community

---

## Design Philosophy

STC isn't designed to look like another generic technology platform.

The website follows an **editorial, minimal, human-centered visual language** built around:

- Strong typography
- Generous whitespace
- Warm off-white and charcoal tones
- Editorial layouts
- Scroll-driven storytelling
- Subtle interactions
- Smooth transitions
- Purposeful motion
- Responsive compositions

The goal is to make the website feel **alive without becoming visually noisy**.

No unnecessary gradients.  
No excessive cards.  
No generic dashboard layouts.  
No visual clutter.

Just a focused digital experience built around the community.
